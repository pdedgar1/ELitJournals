# Premio Itaú de Artes Visuales — Winners, Premiación Jury & Organizers (2009–2023)

Source note: the prize's official site (fundacionitau.org.ar) is offline (DNS failure, confirmed directly). This file was compiled from press coverage (Infobae, LA NACION, Ámbito, Infoarte, El Destape, Diario de Cultura, cultura.gob.ar), art-world outlets (ARTEINFORMADO, Hipermedula, Arte-Online, ramona), a third-party curatorial archive (curaduriaprofesional.com), and — where nothing else survived — Wayback Machine snapshots of the dead official site. Per scope, each header lists only the prize winners, the small "jurado de premiación" (the jury that decides the winners, not the larger ~15–24-person "jurado de selección" that narrows finalists), and curators/coordinators/organizers. Editions where winner names could not be recovered anywhere online are marked **[gap]** in their description — coverage of the earliest years (2009–2013) and of 2016–2017 appears to have mostly vanished along with the official site.

---
