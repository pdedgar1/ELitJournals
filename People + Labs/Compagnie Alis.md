ALIS was founded in 1982 by [[Pierre Fourny]]. From 1985 to 2008, Pierre Fourny and [[Dominique Soria]] developed the artistic projects of ALIS.[[Christophe Marquis]] was the administrative management of ALIS from 1987 to 2004. [[Chantal Le Vourch]] assisted him from 1999 and took over until 2006. [[Hélène Caubel]] directed the production of ALIS' artistic and educational projects from 2012 to 2019. [[Garance Roggero]] and [[Elise Michelet]] administered ALIS from 2006 to 2012, then [[Camille Janodet]] from 2013 to 2017, [[Justine Rousseau]] in 2018-2019, occasionally, [[Nicolas Gilles]] in 2022 and 2023, and [[Camille Ghestin]] from 2019 to 2024. [[Alexandre Liverneaux]] was in charge of multimedia designs and realizations from 2001 to 2011.
