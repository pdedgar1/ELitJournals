#organization 
 The following text is an article based on a series of Panel discussions held online from January 2021 to March 2022.   
The contributors are: [[Andrew Klobucar]], [[Dene Grigar]], [[M. D. Coverley aka Marjorie C. Luesebrink]], [[Stuart Moulthrop]], [[Jim Andrews]], [[Jesper Juul]], [[Patrick Jagoda]], [[Alan Sondheim]], [[Nick Montfort]], [[Gregory L. Ulmer]], [[Søren Bro Pold]], [[Mark Bernstein]], [[Andrew Plotkin]], [[Annie Abrahams]], [[Florian Cramer]], [[Teo Spiller]], [[Sandy Baldwin]], [[Erik Zepka]]. Panel sessions hosted by Kirill Azernyi. This material is based on a diverse discussion of digital phenomenology and the role of practical knowledge in the perception of digital arts.  
Panel sessions began in January 2021 and involved a number of scholars and artists connected by theory and practice of digital arts.   
Core agenda of the seminar can be described briefly by stating a question: How does interaction change aesthetic experience in digital arts?
