Electropolis

The city of electronic-literature venues. Drag to orbit · drag up/down to change camera height · scroll to zoom. Towers are venues (height = contributors, colour = folder type); roads are the routes between districts. Dotted threads link each conference series (ELO, E-Poetry, ACM Hypertext, ICIDS, Narrascope) in year order. Click a district to see just its roads; click its ● to hide it.
