◈ Electropolis

A city built from the network. Each district is a detected community (Louvain), sized by its population. Each building is a person, sized by how many venues they appear in. Streets connect neighbours who share venues; glowing highways are the major routes between districts. Hover to explore, click a district to isolate it.

Districts (communities)

Building size = venue footprint. Street = shared venue (thicker = stronger). Highway = major inter-district route; hover it for the lead bridge person.
